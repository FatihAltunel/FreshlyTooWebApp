Project: FreshlyToo Mobile App Landing Page (v2 - Professional Brand Redesign)
Role: Product Manager
Target Audience: Eco-conscious consumers & food-service businesses (restaurants, bakeries).
Main Message: "Save surplus food, stop waste." ("Fazla yemeği kurtar, israfı durdur.")

#### 1. Page Sections & Architecture (6 Core Sections)
The landing page must guide users through a professional single-page flow using the following 6 core sections:
1. **Home / Hero (Discover):** Impact headline, live metrics, primary waitlist CTA, and main app screen preview (`home.png`).
2. **For Businesses (İşletmeler):** B2B solution, revenue conversion for unsold surplus, and restaurant panel preview.
3. **Pricing (Fiyatlandırma):** 3-tier structure (Consumer Free, Business Starter 0 ₺, Business Pro 99 ₺).
4. **Our Story (Hikâyemiz):** Mission, vision, and environmental philosophy.
5. **FAQ (SSS):** 8 core accordion questions addressing user & business concerns.
6. **Contact / Waitlist (İletişim):** Email capture, waitlist form, social links, and media kit.

#### 2. Key UX and Functional Constraints
* **Visual-First Strategy:** Heavy blocks of text must be minimized. Real mobile app screenshots (`home.png`, `map.png`, `detail.png`, `pickup-qr.png`) from the design handoff package must be embedded inside device mockups.
* **Localization:** Full bilingual support (Turkish - TR, English - EN) managed via an application-wide language switcher in the Header.
